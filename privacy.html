WordDeck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

WordDeck is a language-learning web app. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture provided by Google.
  • Learning data: your decks, words, phrases, progress, settings and learning path.
  • Gemini API key: the key you enter yourself to enable AI features.

How we use it

Data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, save your progress and personalize your lessons. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where data is stored

Account and learning data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider. When you use AI features, the text of your requests is sent to Google's Gemini API using your own key and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we only receive your basic profile information (email, name, picture). We do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Deleting your data

You can export your data or delete your account at any time in the app settings. Deleting your account permanently removes your data from our database.
